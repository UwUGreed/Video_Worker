WIBTA: MIL Creating AI Photos of Me  My husband and I have been together for five years. My MIL has always been very into posting on Facebook (at least one post a day). She even used to post for my husband when he was a kid/teen. Very often, when we send her photos of ourselves, what we’re doing, or anything noteworthy, they get posted on Facebook without our knowledge. Sometimes I’ll log onto Facebook and see photos I had no idea she had taken of us. She has around 2,000 followers and posts everything completely publicly.  It has always made me uncomfortable, but I never thought it was a real problem until now. My husband and I have been busy with new jobs and haven’t been able to make the trip to see his parents together since the holidays. He’s gone without me, but I have an odd work schedule.  Yesterday, I got a call from my parents asking if I had made the trip to see his family. When I checked Facebook, I saw that my MIL had created an AI-generated photo of us watching a football game with them, all wearing matching jerseys. This bothered my husband as well, and he offered to text his mom and ask her not to create any more AI photos of us.  I’ve always had a great relationship with my MIL, and she has always been very kind and understanding, so neither of us expected her to react poorly. She did. She told us we were ungrateful, that we have always hurt her feelings when we ask for no pictures, and that we—and especially me—are too sensitive and should just let her post whatever she wants.  My husband is also uncomfortable with how she’s acting and draws the line at AI photos, but says we can deal with her normal posting habits. I thought I had a say before in what gets posted, but now I’m uncomfortable with the entire situation.  Would I be the asshole if I started telling my MIL not to post pictures of me (outside of family photos and reasonable situations)?
